# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and technically constructive.
- Assume good faith while asking for evidence.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Respect copyright, licenses and tester privacy.
- Keep disagreements focused on code, data and reproducible behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or pressuring contributors to share copyrighted assets is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or private vulnerability-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public communities.
